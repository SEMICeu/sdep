<h1>Definitions</h1>

This document provides SDEP definitions, in alphabetical order.

*Informal definitions, for conceptual purposes only. They are simplified interpretations used to describe the functional architecture and should not be treated as the formal legal definitions set out in [Regulation (EU) 2024/1028](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1028/oj/eng).*

## Activity

An ongoing or completed short-term rental booked through a short-term rental platform.

## Activity monitoring

The process of overseeing compliance with activity regulations.

## Activity monitoring authority (AMA)

An authority responsible for monitoring compliance with activity regulations.

This authority is typically a statistics authority (STA).

*In SDEP-NL: Inspectie Leefomgeving en Transport (ILT).*

## Activity regulation

The process of regulating rental activities in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2024/1028.

## Address

The physical location of a property associated with a listing or activity.

## Area

The geographical zone in which an address is located, within the context of a listing or activity.

## Competent authority (CA)

An authority responsible for enforcing regulations (listing, activity) in designated regulated areas for short-term rentals.

## Host

An individual who offers a private residence for short-term rental.

## Listing

A short-term rental offering published on a short-term rental platform.

## Listing monitoring

The process of overseeing compliance with listing regulations.

## Listing monitoring authority (LMA)

An authority responsible for monitoring compliance with listing regulations.

*In SDEP-NL: Autoriteit Consument & Markt (ACM).*

## Listing regulation

The process of regulating listings in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2024/1028.

## Listing screening authority (LSA)

An internal SDEP component that screens the listings submitted by platforms (random checks) and raises flags, for example when a registration number is unknown or expired.

*In SDEP-NL: part of the SDEP implementation, optionally backed by an external registration system.*

## Online travel agency (OTA)

A synonym for Short-Term Rental Platform.

## Registration system (RS)

A system in which short-term rental addresses are registered and assigned a unique registration number.

*In SDEP-NL, there are multiple RSs, from which one is the Registratiesysteem Toeristische Verhuur (RVV).*

## Regulated area

An area subject to Regulation (EU) 2024/1028.

## Short-term rental

A furnished private residence that can be rented out for a limited period, rather than a hotel or hostel.

*Sometimes also used as shorthand "STR".*

## Short-term rental platform (STR)

A platform used to list and book short-term rentals.

*Always used as shorthand "STR".*

## Statistics authority (STA)

An authority responsible for statistics.

*In SDEP-NL: Centraal Bureau voor de Statistiek (CBS).*

*In the EU: EuroStat.*
